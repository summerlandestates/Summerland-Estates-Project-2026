Summerland Estates is an online marketplace that makes it easy for homeowners, estate
owners, businesses, recruiters, and home service professionals to find one another,
communicate, and build working relationships.
Our goal is simple:
Make finding the right people for the home easier.
Whether you're searching for an Estate Manager, Housekeeper, Private Chef, Mobile Dog
Groomer, Window Cleaner, Groundskeeper, Nanny, Caregiver, Driver, Handyman, or another
trusted home professional, Summerland Estates provides one centralized place to connect.
What Summerland Estates Is
Summerland Estates is a technology platform.
We provide tools that allow users to:
● Create professional profiles
● Search for professionals
● Search for homeowners and employers
● Post jobs and employment opportunities
● Apply for jobs
● Send private messages
● Upload photos and portfolios
● Share work experience
● Display optional verification badges
● Build professional relationships
● Network within the home services industry
We simply provide the platform that helps people find one another.
What Summerland Estates Is Not
Summerland Estates is not:
● An employer
● A staffing agency
● An employment agency
● A recruiting agency
● A broker
● A referral service
● A management company
● A contractor
● A payroll company
● A property management company
● A home service provider
We do not hire professionals.
We do not employ professionals.
We do not supervise professionals.
We do not assign work.
We do not negotiate compensation.
We do not recommend one professional over another.
We do not guarantee results.
We do not guarantee safety.
We do not guarantee qualifications.
How Hiring Works
Hiring decisions are made entirely between users.
Homeowners decide:
● Who to interview
● Who to hire
● What to pay
● When work begins
● What services are performed
● Whether someone is the right fit
Professionals decide:
● Which opportunities to pursue
● Which clients to work with
● Their own pricing (where applicable)
● Their availability
● Whether to accept work
Summerland Estates does not participate in hiring decisions.
Homeowners
If you are hiring someone through Summerland Estates, you are responsible for making your
own decisions.
We encourage homeowners to:
● Conduct interviews
● Request references
● Verify licenses when applicable
● Verify insurance
● Review experience
● Ask questions
● Determine whether someone is appropriate for their household
Every home is different.
Every hiring decision is different.
Only you can determine who is the best fit for your needs.
Professionals
Professionals are responsible for maintaining accurate profiles.
This includes:
● Work history
● Experience
● Certifications
● Licenses
● Insurance
● Photos
● Availability
● Contact information
Professionals are responsible for complying with all applicable federal, state, and local laws.
Companies
Companies may create business profiles and advertise employment opportunities.
Companies are responsible for:
● Job descriptions
● Employment eligibility
● Wages
● Payroll
● Taxes
● Insurance
● Compliance with employment laws
● Hiring decisions
Summerland Estates does not employ workers on behalf of companies.
Recruiters
Recruiters may use Summerland Estates to identify qualified candidates and advertise
opportunities.
Recruiters are responsible for ensuring their recruiting activities comply with all applicable laws
and industry standards.
Identity Verification
Some users may choose to verify their identity through one of our third-party verification
partners.
Identity verification simply confirms that the verification process was successfully completed.
It does not guarantee:
● Character
● Honesty
● Professional ability
● Licensing
● Safety
● Future conduct
Verification should be considered one factor among many when evaluating a professional.
Background Checks
Some users may choose to complete an optional background screening through one of our
independent third-party providers.
A completed background check means only that the screening process was completed.
It should not be interpreted as:
● An endorsement
● A recommendation
● A guarantee
● A certification of safety
Homeowners should make independent hiring decisions based on their own needs and
evaluation.
Reviews
Reviews represent the personal opinions and experiences of individual users.
Reviews are not endorsements by Summerland Estates.
We encourage honest, respectful, and constructive feedback.
Payments
Unless expressly stated otherwise, payment arrangements are made directly between users.
Summerland Estates is generally not a party to agreements regarding compensation, wages,
pricing, scheduling, or services performed.
Safety First
Your safety is important.
We encourage every user to:
● Meet in safe environments when appropriate
● Verify identities
● Request references
● Confirm insurance and licenses where applicable
● Trust your judgment
● Report suspicious activity
If something doesn't feel right, don't move forward.
Reporting Concerns
If you believe a profile, job posting, review, or message violates our policies, please report it
through the platform.
Our team reviews reports and may investigate, remove content, suspend accounts, or take
other appropriate action.
Our Mission
Summerland Estates exists to make it easier for homeowners, estate owners, professionals,
companies, and recruiters to connect.
We believe finding trusted home professionals should be simple, transparent, and efficient.
By bringing the entire home services community together in one place, we hope to create more
opportunities, stronger professional relationships, and better hiring experiences for everyone.
Our Promise
We believe finding great people shouldn't feel overwhelming.
That's why Summerland Estates is committed to building a platform that is simple to use,
transparent, respectful of your privacy, and focused on helping people make informed decisions.
We'll continue improving our technology, introducing new tools, and listening to feedback from
homeowners, professionals, companies, and recruiters as we grow.
Our role is to make meaningful connections possible. Your role is to choose the people
who are right for you.
